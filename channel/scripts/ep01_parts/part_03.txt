Aurex is the number one hero in the world. Crown rank. Spark Score 998. They call him the Golden Wall, because no monster has ever gotten past him.

He's also the face of the Bureau. Every billboard, every cereal box, every TV ad. When people imagine the word hero, they imagine Aurex.

And according to Mira's map, Aurex has been at the scene of nineteen of the last twenty attacks.

"Maybe he's just fast," you say.

"Or maybe," Mira says, "he knows where they're going to appear before they appear."

That night, Mira hacks into the Bureau's old monster-research files. Most of it is locked. But one folder isn't, because whoever hid it never imagined a Silver-rank nobody would go looking.

The folder is called "Project Lantern."

Inside are photos. Breach points, the holes monsters come out of, but these ones have metal frames built around them. Machines. Bureau logos.

The Bureau isn't just fighting the monsters.

It's opening the doors for them.

You have proof. You have Mira. And you have the strongest punch in the city.

You should be unstoppable.

But then something goes wrong. Something you never saw coming.

Remember the little girl from the train station? Her dad filmed part of it from the stairs. A shaky, blurry, twelve-second clip. And for the first time, you can see it clearly: a red t-shirt, a white bolt, and a monster flying through a roof.

The internet goes insane.

"WAS IT THE ZERO KID?!" "The Zero is secretly the strongest hero??" "I always knew he was hiding something."

People start cheering for you. Believing in you. Kids draw your bolt on their notebooks. Strangers stop you on the street to shake your hand.

And every single time they do… you feel it.

You're getting weaker.

Every fan, every cheer, every "I believe in you" drains the battery. A week after the video goes viral, you try to punch a parking meter, and you hurt your hand.

For the first time in your life, the whole city loves you.

And it's killing your power.

Then Aurex makes his move.

At 6:14 PM on a Friday, the biggest breach in forty years opens in the middle of Meridian City. And out of it climbs something that makes every monster before it look like a pet.

Eclipse level. The Bureau names it Gorrak. It's taller than the buildings around it. Its roar knocks people off their feet three blocks away.

Every phone in the city buzzes with the same alert: "EVACUATE. ECLIPSE-LEVEL THREAT. CROWN HEROES DISPATCHED."

And then Aurex appears. Floating above the city, golden armour shining, every news camera pointed at him. This is the moment. The biggest hero moment in history.

But instead of fighting… he talks.

"People of Meridian City," he says. "Before I save you tonight, you deserve the truth. There's a lie going around about a so-called hero. The Zero. A boy with no power, no rank and no talent, who's been stealing credit from real heroes."

"He's a fraud. A nobody. The weakest hero ever scanned. And tonight, while he hides, I'll show you what a real hero looks like."

Mira grabs your arm. "He knows," she whispers. "He found the video. He's trying to make the city turn on you before you can tell them about Project Lantern."

But you're not looking at her. You're looking at your hands.

Because all across the city, people are staring at their screens. Millions of them. And millions of them are starting to think the same thing:

Maybe Aurex is right. Maybe the Zero really is nothing.

Aurex doesn't know your rule.

He just told the entire city you're the weakest person alive.

He just gave you the biggest charge of your life.

Gorrak sees you. All three eyes lock onto the tiny figure in the red t-shirt.

Aurex sees you too. For a second, his smile slips. "Go home, Zero," he says into the cameras. "You're embarrassing yourself."

Thank you, you think.

Gorrak roars and brings one giant claw down, straight at you.

You don't dodge. You don't block.

You punch.

The shockwave rolls down the avenue. Every car alarm in the city goes off at once. And Gorrak, an Eclipse-level monster that was supposed to destroy Meridian City, is a fading purple dot in the sky.

And it doesn't stop there.

The force of the punch rips the breach wide open, and underneath it everyone sees what's buried there: a Bureau machine, glowing purple, with the Bureau logo stamped on the side.

Every news camera in the city is pointed right at it. Live. In 4K.

Mira's drones are already uploading the Project Lantern files to every screen in Meridian City.

The Golden Wall just watched his whole lie fall apart.

By morning, it's over. Aurex is arrested. The Bureau's leaders are under investigation. Project Lantern is shut down, and for the first time in years, Meridian City goes a full month without a single monster attack.

And the city knows the truth now. The Zero is real.

The new Bureau director offers you everything. Crown rank. Number one. Your own billboard. Your face on every cereal box in the country.
