What happens if you wake up in a world where giant monsters attack every single week… where heroes are ranked like celebrities, and every eighteen-year-old gets a number that decides their whole life… and your number is zero.

Everyone calls you the weakest hero alive. Your own rank card says it. The news says it. Even the kids on your street say it.

But there's one thing nobody in this city knows about you.

And by the end of today, the strongest hero in the world is going to find out the hard way.

Before we start, if you like stories where you are the main character, hit subscribe. Every week, we drop you into a brand-new world.

Now… let's go.

In this world, monsters don't hide in movies. They come out of the ground.

Nobody knows exactly where they come from. Scientists call them breach creatures. Everyone else just calls them monsters. Some are the size of a dog. Some are the size of a building. And every single one gets a threat level.

Ember level: dangerous, but a couple of police officers can handle it. Storm level: it can flatten a street. You need a hero. Quake level: it can wipe out a whole district. Eclipse level: it can end a city. And Extinction level… nobody likes to talk about Extinction level. The last one was forty years ago, and the map still has a hole where a city used to be.

So the world needed protection. And that's how the Bureau was born.

The Bureau of Registered Heroes runs everything. It trains heroes, pays heroes, ranks heroes, and, most importantly, sells heroes. Hero merchandise. Hero TV shows. Hero energy drinks. In Meridian City, heroes aren't just protectors. They're the biggest celebrities on the planet.

And every hero has a rank.

Iron is the bottom. Iron heroes rescue cats and direct traffic. Bronze and Silver handle the everyday monsters. Gold heroes get the billboards. And Crown… there are only seven Crown heroes in the entire world. They're treated like gods.

So how does the Bureau decide who becomes what?

Simple. On your eighteenth birthday, you walk into a Bureau center and put your hand on a machine called the Scanner. It reads the energy inside your body and gives you a number between zero and one thousand. Your Spark Score.

Score above a hundred? Congratulations, you're a hero. Score above five hundred? You're going to be famous. Score above nine hundred? You'll be Crown rank before you're twenty-five.

That number is your whole future. Your job. Your money. How people look at you on the street.

Which brings us to you.

Four years ago, on your eighteenth birthday, you walked into the Bureau center with your mom. You were wearing your favourite red t-shirt, the one with the little white lightning bolt. You'd drawn that bolt yourself when you were nine, because you wanted to be a hero so badly it hurt.

You put your hand on the Scanner.

The lights started to climb… and then stopped. Right at the bottom.

Zero.

Not five. Not one. Zero. The technician actually laughed. He said in twenty years he'd never seen a real zero. He even took a photo of the screen.

By the next morning, that photo was everywhere. "The Zero Kid." "The Most Powerless Human Ever Scanned." People made memes. Someone made a song.

The Bureau still gave you a hero license, because technically anyone who applies gets one. But they made you Iron rank, number 4,812. The very last hero in the entire city.

So for four years, that's what you've been. You help old ladies cross the street. You get cats out of trees. Once, you spent six hours guarding a parking lot because a Gold hero was filming a commercial there.

And every day, someone reminds you what you are.

"Hey, Zero, saved any cats today?" "Careful, Zero, don't hurt yourself."

You smile, you wave, you keep walking.

Because here's the thing. Deep down, there's a feeling you've never told anyone about. Every time someone laughs at you, your hands get warm. Like something inside you is… waking up.

You always thought it was just anger.

You were wrong.

It's a Tuesday. You're at Central Station, carrying groceries for Mrs. Delgado from the fourth floor, when the ground starts shaking.

Then the floor of Platform 6 explodes.

A Storm-level monster. The kind that needs a Silver hero at least. Alarms are screaming. People are running in every direction. Somewhere a kid is crying for her dad.

And the nearest registered hero is… you.

You check your phone. The Bureau app says: "Silver hero dispatched. Estimated arrival: 9 minutes."

Nine minutes. In nine minutes, this thing can tear the whole station apart.

The monster turns. It sees the little girl, standing alone by a pillar. It opens a mouth full of teeth the size of kitchen knives.

You don't think. You just run.

You jump in front of the girl. The monster's claw comes down. And as you raise your arm to protect your face, you hear someone behind you on the platform shout:

"Get out of the way, Zero! You're useless!"

And your hands get warm.

No. Not warm.

They burn.
