# Tutorial Video Script: "Is Viral Channel Ka Animation Style Reveal + FREE Skill"

**Format:** Faceless screen-recording + voiceover (Hinglish)
**Length:** ~14–16 min
**Chapters (description mein daalo):**
00:00 Hook – ye channel dekho
01:10 Free skill + niche ka offer
01:50 Poora process ek nazar mein
02:40 Step 1 – Idea aur niche
04:00 Step 2 – Script kaise likhi
05:40 Step 3 – Voiceover (ElevenLabs)
06:50 Step 4 – Claude guide (step by step)
10:00 Claude ka result (1–2 min)
12:00 Step 5 – Gemini guide (step by step)
14:00 Gemini ka result (1–2 min)
15:30 Sabse badi galti jo maine ki (sync)
16:10 Free skill kaise lo + outro

> **[SCREEN]** = screen pe kya dikhana hai · **[VO]** = jo bolna hai · `[ ]` brackets = tumhe apni detail bharni hai

---

## 00:00 – HOOK (channel reveal)

**[SCREEN]** Channel ka screenshot (jo image tum bhejoge), slow zoom-in. Views wale numbers pe red circle aur arrow.

**[VO]**
"Guys, ye channel dekho. [Channel ka naam]. Iski videos pe [X lakh / X million] views aa rahe hain. Aur dhyan se dekho, isme koi chehra nahi hai, koi studio nahi hai, sirf 2D animation hai.

Maine pure YouTube pe dhoonda. Is animation style ka ek bhi proper tutorial nahi mila. Koi nahi batata ki ye banta kaise hai.

Toh aaj main pehli baar is animation style ko poora reveal karne wala hoon, step by step, A se Z tak."

**[SCREEN]** Quick 5-second montage of your own EP01 clips: hero waking up, the monster smash, "ZERO" stamp.

**[VO]**
"Aur ye jo tum dekh rahe ho, ye video maine khud banayi hai. Bina kisi animator ke, bina After Effects ke. Poori AI se."

---

## 01:10 – FREE OFFER

**[SCREEN]** Text pop: "FREE SKILL" + "FREE NICHE"

**[VO]**
"Aur sabse badi baat: is video mein main tumhe do cheezein bilkul free de raha hoon.

Pehli, meri khud ki **skill**, jisse ye animation banti hai. Tum use download karke seedha use kar sakte ho.

Doosri, ek **niche** jisme abhi competition bahut kam hai aur demand bahut zyada.

Link description mein hai. Bas video end tak dekhna, kyunki beech mein ek galti bataunga jo maine ki thi. Agar tumne wo galti ki toh poori video kharab ho jayegi."

> **Note:** "101% viral" mat bolna. YouTube aur viewers dono guaranteed-result claims ko pasand nahi karte, aur ye misleading lagta hai. Iski jagah bolo: "Is style mein videos ka chance bahut accha hai, kyunki is pe abhi koi kaam nahi kar raha."

---

## 01:50 – PROCESS EK NAZAR MEIN

**[SCREEN]** Ek simple 5-step diagram, har step ek-ek karke pop-in:
1. Idea / Niche → 2. Script → 3. Voiceover → 4. Animation (Claude ya Gemini) → 5. Final video

**[VO]**
"Poora process bas 5 steps ka hai. Idea, script, voice, animation, aur final video.

Aur main tumhe animation ke do tareeke dikhaunga. Ek **Claude** wala, jo maine is video ke liye use kiya. Aur ek **Gemini** wala. Dono ke steps lagbhag same hain, toh jiske paas jo tool hai, wo use kar sakta hai."

---

## 02:40 – STEP 1: IDEA AUR NICHE

**[SCREEN]** Claude chat: tumhara pehla message, jisme tumne channel ka idea diya.

**[VO]**
"Sabse pehle maine Claude ko bola: 'YouTube ki faceless channel wali saari policies padho.' Kyunki 2026 mein YouTube 'inauthentic content' pe bahut strict hai. Agar tum copy-paste ya low-effort content daaloge, toh monetization nahi milega.

Phir maine apna idea diya: **'In This World'** type POV stories. Jaise wo viral Roblox wali 'In this town' videos hoti hain. Har video ek nayi duniya, aur viewer khud main character.

Lekin ek cheez dhyan rakhna: maine kisi anime ki story copy nahi ki. World ka vibe anime jaisa hai, par story, naam, ranks aur monsters sab apne hain. Isse copyright ka koi chakkar nahi."

**[SCREEN]** Free niche reveal card.

**[VO]**
"Aur yahi hai tumhara **free niche**: *[apna niche yahan likho, jaise 'What happens if you… POV anime-world stories, 2D style']*. Isme abhi high-quality 2D animation wala koi bhi nahi hai."

---

## 04:00 – STEP 2: SCRIPT KAISE LIKHI

**[SCREEN]** Script file scroll, jisme hook, world explain aur ranks wale hisse highlight hon.

**[VO]**
"Ab script. Maine Claude ko ye rules diye:

Ek: start mein **'What happens if you…'** wala hook, aur shuru mein kuch bhi reveal nahi karna.
Do: hook ke turant baad **subscribe** bolna.
Teen: phir **world explain** karna: is duniya mein monsters hain, heroes ki ranking hai, Spark Score hai.
Chaar: poori story apne style mein.
Paanch: end mein viewers se poochna ki agli video kis world pe chahiye.

Claude ne pehle poora **world bible** banaya: city ka naam, ranks Iron se Crown tak, monsters ke threat levels, characters. Phir uske hisaab se 20–30 minute ki script likhi."

**[SCREEN]** Prompt on screen (copyable):
```
Write a 20–30 min second-person POV story script for my channel "In This World".
Rules: open with a "What happens if you…" hook and reveal nothing; ask to subscribe
right after the hook; explain the world's rules early; original names, ranks and
monsters only (anime vibe, no copied plots); end by asking which world viewers want next.
First give me a world bible, then the full narration in paragraphs.
```

**[VO]**
"Ek pro tip: script ko **5000 characters ke parts** mein todwa lena. Kyunki ElevenLabs ek baar mein itna hi leta hai, aur parts mein video banana bhi aasaan hota hai."

---

## 05:40 – STEP 3: VOICEOVER (ELEVENLABS)

**[SCREEN]** ElevenLabs dashboard: voice select, text paste, generate.

**[VO]**
"Voice ke liye maine **ElevenLabs** use kiya. Storytelling ke liye deep aur calm English voice acchi lagti hai. Maine *[voice ka naam, jaise Brian ya Chris]* li.

Har part alag se generate karo aur MP3 download kar lo.

Dhyan do: agar tum monetize karna chahte ho, toh ElevenLabs ka **paid plan** lo. Free plan pe commercial use allowed nahi hai."

## 06:50 – STEP 4: CLAUDE GUIDE (STEP BY STEP)

**[SCREEN]** Har step ka screen recording.

**[VO]**
"Ab asli magic: animation. Pehle Claude wala tareeka."

**Step 4.1: Skill install karo**
**[SCREEN]** Claude settings mein skills section, phir skill file upload.
**[VO]** "Description wali free skill download karo. Claude mein Settings ke andar Skills section mein jaake usse upload kar do. Bas, ab Claude ko pata hai ki ye ink-line 2D style kaise banana hai: round head characters, moti black outline, night lighting, camera movement, sab kuch."

**Step 4.2: Reference do**
**[SCREEN]** Reference video ya screenshot upload karte hue.
**[VO]** "Phir Claude ko apni pasand ke style ka ek reference dikhao aur bolo: 'Is type ki 2D animation chahiye. Har scene mein movement ho, aur aisa lage jaise haath se bani ho.'"

**Step 4.3: Pehle 10 second ka preview**
**[SCREEN]** Pehla preview, phir premium wala preview, side by side.
**[VO]** "Seedha poori video mat banwao. Pehle **10 second ka preview** banwao. Mera pehla preview theek tha, par premium nahi lag raha tha. Toh maine bola: 'Isko premium karo. Characters acche lagein, sone wala scene accha lage, fight scenes acche lagein.' Aur dekho fark."

**[SCREEN]** Show premium details: blanket breathing, impact frame, film grain.

**Step 4.4: Voiceover upload karo, poora part banwao**
**[SCREEN]** MP3 upload, aur prompt on screen.
```
Here is the voiceover for Part 1. Make the full part in this 2D style.
Lock every shot to the words: transcribe the voiceover with word timestamps first,
then start each shot on the first word of its sentence. Movement in every shot,
characters talking on their lines, anime-style fight scenes.
```
**[VO]** "Ab apna voiceover upload karo aur ye prompt do. Claude har sentence ke liye ek alag scene banata hai. Meri pehli video mein lagbhag 100 shots the."

**Step 4.5: Har sentence ka frame check karwao**
**[VO]** "Render se pehle Claude se bolo: 'Har sentence ka ek frame nikaal ke check karo ki screen pe wahi aa raha hai jo voice bol rahi hai.' Is ek line se aadhi problems wahin khatam ho jaati hain."

**Step 4.6: Parts jodo**
**[VO]** "Part 2 ka voiceover do aur bolo: 'Part 2 banao aur Part 1 ke saath jod do, Part 1 dobara mat banana.' Aur bas, poori video ready."

## 10:00 – CLAUDE KA RESULT (1–2 min)

- Station fight: floor ka phatna, hero ka daudna, "They burn"

## 15:30 – SABSE BADI GALTI (sync)

**[SCREEN]** Pehli wali out-of-sync video ka clip, jisme voice aage aur scene peeche ho. Upar red "❌". Phir fixed version, upar green "✅".

**[VO]**
"Ab wo galti jo maine bataya tha. Meri pehli video mein voice aage chal rahi thi aur scene peeche aa rahe the. Kahin 7 second ka farak tha.

Wajah ye thi ki scenes ko voice ke pauses se andaaze se lagaya gaya tha.

Fix simple hai: AI ko bolo ki **pehle voiceover ko word timestamps ke saath transcribe kare**, aur har scene ko us sentence ke pehle shabd pe lock kare. Ye ek line yaad rakh lo, tumhari video kabhi out of sync nahi hogi."
