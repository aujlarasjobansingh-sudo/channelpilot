# Channel Plan — Novel Story Explainer (2D animated)

Status: style + first topic not chosen yet (Nov 19, 2026).

## Format (every video, 20–30 min)
1. **Hook (0:00–0:45):** fast montage of the story's biggest moments as questions —
   "Ek aadmi jise shaadi ke din jail bhej diya gaya... 14 saal baad wo wapas aaya." Never give the ending.
2. **Subscribe ask (0:45–0:55):** one short line, right before the story starts.
3. **Story in 4–5 acts:** retold in our own voice and order. Same plot, but more drama, clear
   build-up, cliffhanger at every act end (re-hook every 3–5 min).
4. **Value points inside the story:** short pauses: "Yahan dhyaan do...", the hidden meaning,
   history behind it, life lesson. These make it ours, not just a summary.
5. **Ending + 3 takeaways + next video tease.**

## Visual style options
- **A. Midnight Ink (recommended):** dark navy paper, layered cutout parallax, warm lantern light,
  faceless silhouette characters with one accent colour each.
- **B. Woodcut Comic:** bold engraving linework + 2 flat colours, comic-panel transitions.
- **C. Chalk & Ember:** hand-drawn chalk on slate, glowing ember highlights.
