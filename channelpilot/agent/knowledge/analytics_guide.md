# Analytics Guide

## Metrics that matter
- **Views** — raw reach. Compare against your own average, not others'.
- **Unique viewers** — how many different people; high views with low uniques = loyal core.
- **Impressions & CTR** — how often YouTube showed it and how often people clicked.
  ⚠️ NOT available through the YouTube API. Check manually in YouTube Studio > Analytics > Reach.
- **Average view duration (AVD)** — how long people watch on average.
- **Average percentage viewed (APV)** — what share of the video they watch. The retention metric.
- **Watch time** — total minutes. The core satisfaction proxy.
- **Subscribers gained/lost** — net growth per video/period.
- **Traffic sources** — where views came from (browse, suggested, search, Shorts, external).
- **Returning vs new viewers** — loyal audience vs discovery. Both matter; imbalance tells a story.

## Healthy benchmarks (rough)
- CTR: 2–10%. Under ~3% usually means the packaging needs work. Over 6% is strong.
- APV: 40–60%+ is good for most formats; under 30% means retention problems.
- Returning viewers: 20–40% is a healthy mix for a growing channel.

## Reading traffic sources
- **Browse-heavy**: packaging is working, home feed likes you. Protect the format.
- **Suggested-heavy**: your videos pair well with others' — make more on adjacent topics.
- **Search-heavy**: evergreen SEO working. Keep publishing searchable topics.
- **Shorts-heavy, subs flat**: Shorts viewers rarely subscribe or watch long-form. Funnel them.

## Diagnosing a drop
1. Did packaging change (new thumbnail style, vaguer titles)?
2. Topic shift? New topics reset audience expectations.
3. Seasonality (holidays, exams, big events stealing attention)?
4. Shorts cannibalizing long-form watch time?
5. One outlier video skewing the average? Compare medians, not means.
6. Check Studio > Reach for CTR and impressions — a CTR collapse points at thumbnails/titles,
   an impressions collapse points at topic/audience mismatch.

## How to review (cadence)
- Weekly: glance at latest videos vs channel average.
- Monthly: judge properly. One month of data beats one week of panic.
- Per video: CTR + retention in the first 48 hours tells you if packaging worked.
