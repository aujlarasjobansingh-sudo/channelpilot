# Faceless Channel Policy Guide (researched Nov 2026)

Sources: YouTube Help (monetization policies, expanded YPP), Tubefilter, Search Engine Journal,
TechTimes, ppc.land, plus creator blogs. Official pages could not be opened directly when this
was written. Re-check YouTube Help / YouTube Studio before any big decision.

## 1. Faceless is allowed
No rule says a human must be on camera. Voiceover, stock footage, animation, screen recording
and AI tools are all fine. YouTube punishes LOW EFFORT and MASS PRODUCTION, not facelessness.

## 2. Monetization: what will NOT be monetized
Review happens at CHANNEL level. If too much of the channel is bad, the WHOLE channel loses
monetization, even good videos.

**Inauthentic content** (renamed from "repetitious content", July 2025; clarified July 2026):
1. **Generic / template content** — same template, swap a few words, mass upload. Slideshows
   with a robot voice reading Wikipedia. Hundreds of near-identical videos.
2. **Unsatisfying or off-putting content** — low-value, disturbing or "AI slop" videos that
   viewers find unsettling or pointless.
3. **AI personas posing as human experts on sensitive topics** — an AI avatar/voice presented
   as a real doctor, lawyer, financial adviser or political expert giving advice.
   AI narration of a well-researched explainer is fine; a fake "expert" telling viewers what
   to do with their health or savings is not.

**Reused content** (separate policy, not about copyright):
- Re-uploads, compilations, clips of movies/TV, reactions with little commentary,
  other creators' videos with tiny edits.
- Allowed ONLY if you add significant original commentary, editing, education or story.
- Having permission or "fair use" does NOT save you from this policy.

## 3. YPP thresholds
**Now (until 31 Jan 2027):**
- Expanded tier (memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping — no ad revenue): 500 subs + 3 public
  uploads in 90 days + (3,000 watch hours in 12 months OR 3M Shorts views in 90 days). India is included.
- Full tier (ad revenue): 1,000 subs + (4,000 watch hours in 12 months OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days).
- Shorts views/hours do NOT count toward long-form watch hours.

**From 1 Feb 2027 (announced Aug 2026), new applicants:**
- 1,000 subs + (8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days OR 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days).
- Existing partners are reported to be grandfathered, but must accept new YPP terms by 31 Jan 2027.
- Shorts revenue sharing reportedly needs 10M qualified Shorts views per rolling 90 days
  (sources disagree on details — verify in Studio).
- **Implication for a new channel:** hitting the old bar before Feb 2027 is unlikely from zero.
  Plan for the 8,000-hour bar. Long-form (8–20 min) builds watch hours far faster than Shorts.

**View counting (from 24 Aug 2026):** public views count from the first frame for all formats.
Money and YPP eligibility use "qualified/engaged" views and watch hours, not public views.

## 4. AI disclosure (altered or synthetic content)
- MUST tick the disclosure in Studio when content is realistic and could be mistaken for real:
  realistic AI people, cloned voice of a real person, fake real events/places, altered footage
  of real events.
- Do NOT need to disclose: AI used for script help, ideas, captions, clearly animated/stylized
  visuals, beauty filters, colour fixes, background blur.
- Since May 2026 YouTube auto-detects and labels AI content, and shows labels more prominently
  (under the player; overlay on Shorts). Not disclosing when required risks removal or
  suspension. When unsure, disclose. Disclosure itself does not hurt monetization.
- Never clone a real person's voice/face without permission.

## 5. Community Guidelines (can get the channel deleted)
Main categories: spam & deceptive practices (fake engagement, misleading metadata/thumbnails,
scams), sensitive content (child safety, nudity, self-harm), violent/dangerous content,
harassment & hate, regulated goods, misinformation (medical, elections), impersonation.
Strike ladder (verify in Studio): first violation is usually a warning (can be cleared with
policy training) → strike 1 = 1-week upload freeze → strike 2 = 2 weeks → 3 strikes in 90 days
= channel terminated. Severe violations skip straight to termination.
Copyright strikes are tracked separately: 3 copyright strikes in 90 days = termination too.

## 6. Copyright for faceless channels
- Music is the #1 trigger. Use YouTube Audio Library, or a paid library whose licence covers
  YouTube monetization (whitelist your channel with them so claims can be cleared).
- Stock footage: read the licence. Pexels/Pixabay are usually fine; never rip footage from
  other YouTubers, films, news channels or TV.
- Claims (automatic, revenue goes to owner) are not strikes, but many claims = no money.
- Thumbnails must also be your own or licensed.

## 7. Faceless channel checklist (stay monetizable)
1. Original script for every video — your own research, angle, story. AI can help draft; a
   human must shape it and fact-check it.
2. Real voice is safest. A good AI voice is allowed, but not an AI "expert persona" on health,
   finance, law or politics.
3. Varied visuals: mix of stock, custom graphics, animations, maps, text — not the same
   slideshow template every time.
4. Each video must be clearly different and worth watching on its own. Quality over volume:
   2–3 strong long videos a week beats 3 template videos a day.
5. Honest titles/thumbnails that match the video (misleading = spam policy).
6. Disclose realistic AI correctly.
7. Only licensed music/footage.
8. Keep a "proof of work" folder (scripts, project files, research notes) — useful if a
   monetization review is appealed.
9. Before applying for YPP, delete or privatize any weak/reused/template videos — reviewers
   look at the whole channel.
