# YouTube SEO Guide

## Titles
- Keep under 60 characters so it doesn't get cut off.
- Put the main keyword near the front.
- Be specific: numbers, timeframes, outcomes ("in 24 hours", "step by step", "full documentary").
- Curiosity gap: promise a payoff without giving it away.
- Match search intent for search videos; match emotion for browse videos.
- Never mislead — mismatch between title and content kills retention and trust.

## Descriptions
- First 150 characters matter most (shown in search). Hook + keywords there.
- Then: 2–4 sentence summary with natural keyword use.
- Add chapters (improves retention and search appearance).
- Links: subscribe link, related videos/playlists, socials.
- Max 3 hashtags at the very end (only the first 3 show above the title).
- 200–400 words is plenty. Don't keyword-stuff; write for humans first.

## Tags
- Tags carry LOW ranking weight now, but still fill them (~300–400 of the 500 characters).
- Mix: 2–3 broad ("documentary"), 5–8 specific ("ancient egypt documentary"),
  a few long-tail phrases ("who built the pyramids documentary").
- Include common misspellings of key terms.
- Never use misleading tags — that's a policy violation.

## Hashtags
- Max 3, and they must be genuinely relevant.
- They show above the title and are clickable topic pages.

## Chapters
- Format: `00:00 Intro` on its own lines, starting at 00:00, minimum 3 chapters, each 10+ seconds.
- Benefit-driven chapter names ("How the pyramids were really built") beat labels ("Part 3").
- Chapters improve retention (viewers jump instead of leaving) and can appear in Google search.

## Captions & files
- Upload accurate captions (or review auto-captions). Helps search, retention, and accessibility.
- Name the video file with keywords before uploading (minor signal, free to do).

## Playlists
- Keyword-rich playlist titles ("Ancient History Documentaries").
- Series playlists keep viewers watching YOUR videos back-to-back (session time).

## End screens, cards, pinned comments
- End screen: next video + subscribe, in the last 20 seconds.
- Cards: link related videos at moments of curiosity.
- Pinned comment: ask a question + soft CTA. The owner replying in comments boosts engagement signals.

## Shorts SEO
- Title works as the hook — make it punchy.
- Description keywords still help. No need for #Shorts (YouTube detects it).

## Never do
- Keyword stuffing, misleading metadata, or tag spam — can get videos removed or the channel struck.
- Copying a big channel's exact titles/tags won't rank you above them.
